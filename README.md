## 📂 Projects

▸ **[Robot-Learning-ADR](https://github.com/Malgesw/Robot-Learning-ADR)**
PPO under Uniform vs Automatic Domain Randomization on MuJoCo's Hopper. ADR led on the harder obstacle task (1035 vs 800 reward) and was the only method robust to a mis-set randomization range. *Course project.*

▸ **[SafetyPredictionForHRC](https://github.com/Malgesw/SafetyPredictionForHRC)**
ST-GCN predicting one second ahead the safety level of an operator working next to a cobot, at 0.063 s mean lead time and 1.6 ms inference. Beat all three baselines on Critical-class F1, and trades a lower F1 against a state-of-the-art model for faster inference and a simpler architecture. *Course (team) project.*

▸ **[VolleyballObjectDetection](https://github.com/Malgesw/VolleyballObjectDetection)**
YOLO detection of court lines, players and ball, with automatic zoom on the ball. Reached 0.995 mAP@50 on court detection, and quantified how far it drops under domain shift. *Course (team) project.*

▸ **[DeepFakeDetection](https://github.com/Malgesw/DeepFakeDetection)**
One-class VAE flagging deepfakes by reconstruction error on FaceForensics++, beating the
OC-FakeDect reference on precision, recall and accuracy with a validation-tuned threshold. *BSc thesis.*
